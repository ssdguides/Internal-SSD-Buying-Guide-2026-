## Don't mix these up

- **Form factor:** physical shape (2.5-inch, M.2 2280)
- **Interface:** SATA or PCIe, which sets the bandwidth ceiling
- **Protocol:** AHCI (SATA) or NVMe (PCIe)
- **PCIe generation:** Gen 3 / 4 / 5, and your slot and CPU must support it

**M.2 is a form factor, not a speed.** Check whether your slot accepts NVMe, SATA, or both.

## Checklist before buying

- [ ] Confirm supported interface, key type and length in your device manual
- [ ] Choose capacity honestly (1TB is the practical floor for most gamers)
- [ ] Leave 10–20% of the drive free
- [ ] Plan cooling (Gen 5 usually needs a heatsink)
- [ ] Compare cost per TB, warranty and endurance, not just headline speed
